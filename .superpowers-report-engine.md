## 1a Long ferry
Route already scaled the track by the driven km, so the ~950 km sea added no length. One real fix was needed:
`_legal_time` counted the FERRY_GAP (barrier to far ramp) as driven road, which added about 2.5 s per ferry. It now
takes the ferries array and skips [z, z_out], so a ferry costs exactly FERRY_STOP_TIME. The new test in
tests/test_ferry.gd uses a fixture road of 1100 km with a ferry from km 50 to km 1000. It checks:
- the same m_per_km as a 150 km road, and a track that road's length plus FERRY_GAP
- legal time equal to the 150 km road's plus FERRY_STOP_TIME (within 0.5 s)
- boards before the quay counting the sea (Iota >= 1070 km)
- after the far quay, the real km left
- mirroring b -> a
- a city 700 km inland reached over the sea (1800 real km, the track of the 850 driven km)

## 1d Biomes

the edge, the feather streaks, and that the new items draw in every season.

## 1e Crimea
Natural Earth 50m has Crimea inside Russia, as a separate ring (RUS polygon 100, 73 points). That ring shares 15
vertices along the Perekop and Chonhar border with Ukraine's mainland. So no hand polygon was needed:
- make_world.py moves the RUS ring that contains (34.10 E, 44.95 N) into Ukraine and merges it with Ukraine's
  mainland (`merge_rings`, as for Cyprus), with no line across. The Kerch peninsula is included; Taman stays Russian.
- It asserts Simferopol is in Ukraine and not in Russia.
- world.json views gained `lonlat`, so Godot can project.
- test_game_data checks that Simferopol's point is in ua and not in ru, Moscow is in ru and Kyiv in ua. I confirmed
  that this check fails on a world.json built without the move.
- Before my change, rebuilding with the current Natural Earth file reproduced the committed world.json byte for byte.
