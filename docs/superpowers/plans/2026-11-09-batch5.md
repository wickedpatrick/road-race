# Batch 5 (Ukraine, Russia, Belarus, Turkey) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Four new countries on their own maps, plus the engine work they need: a long ferry (Kaliningrad), a
suspension bridge stretch (Dardanelles, Bosporus), steppe and taiga landscapes, Crimea as Ukraine on the picker.

**Architecture:** Engine work in one worktree (Task 1, sequential sub-steps, each tested on a fixture country the
way `tests/test_borders.gd` and `tests/test_ferry.gd` do). In parallel, one worktree agent per country (Tasks 2–5),
using existing biomes; Russia writes its Kaliningrad ferry and Turkey its bridges in the formats fixed below. Then
merge one by one (Task 6), switch suitable zones to the new biomes, screenshots at the end.

**Tech Stack:** Godot 4 / GDScript, JSON data in `data/`, tests in `tests/` run headless by `tests/run.sh`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-09-batch5-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Each country on its own map, folders `ua`, `ru`, `by`, `tr`; no routes between countries.
- Ukraine: cities and roads only on government-controlled territory, none occupied or within ~30 km of the line of
  contact (mid-2026). Crimea and occupied areas plain land: no cities, roads, labels. Peacetime look, no war.
- Russia: no Crimea. Kaliningrad only via the ferry Ust-Luga – Baltiysk. No leg over ~1500 km.
- Turkey: the Dardanelles and the Bosporus by bridge (`bridge` field), no ferry; no Northern Cyprus; not a
  neighbour of `cy`.
- Bilingual signs (`native.json`, native on top, Latin under) for `ua`, `ru`, `by`; `name` stays in Latin letters;
  Polish exonyms where they exist (Kijów, Lwów, Mińsk, Grodno, Stambuł) through the existing exonym mechanism.
- Favourite neighbours: ru → by, by → ru, ua → pl, tr → bg.
- Every postcard its own new painter; one postcard per map city; no town postcards; no anthems; texts in all of
  `GameData.LANGUAGES` (pl, en, cs, is, de).
- Anti-goals: no war imagery or "new regions" wording, Crimea never Russian, neutral facts on Kurdish/Armenian/Greek
  history in Turkey.
- **No graphical Godot runs** (game_preview, map_preview, shots.sh, galleries): they steal the user's focus;
  screenshots are taken once at the end by the controller. Headless only (`tests/run.sh`, `--headless` scripts).
- Never delete or overwrite the user's Godot `profile.json`.
- Commit messages in the repo's style (one long English sentence listing what came in), ending with
  `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.

## Review Focus

1. Moscow → Kaliningrad: ~900 km of sea must add no driven length, no time to the limit beyond
   `FERRY_STOP_TIME`, no fuel; boards past the quay show real km. Test in Task 1.
2. Moscow → Vladivostok (~9000 km): track length and time limit sane, autopilot finishes in the limit. Check in
   Task 1 (fixture) and Task 3 (real data).
3. A bridge in the `b -> a` direction and a bridge right after a city: mirrored km, no town sign or station on the
   bridge. Test in Task 1.
4. Simferopol's point falls in Ukraine on the picker, not Russia. Check in Task 1.
5. A town missing from `native.json`: one Latin line, no crash (already tested in batch 4; country agents keep
   `native.json` complete).
