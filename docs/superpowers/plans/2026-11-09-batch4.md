# Batch 4 (Hungary, Romania, Bulgaria, Moldova, Greece, Cyprus, Malta) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Seven new countries on their own maps, plus the engine work they need: ferries, bilingual signs, a
Mediterranean landscape and a whole-island Cyprus on the country picker.

**Architecture:** Engine first (Tasks 1–4, sequential, on `main`), each tested on a tiny fixture country the way
`tests/test_borders.gd` does (`GameData._countries[CODE] = fixture()`), so no real country is needed. Then one
worktree agent per country (Tasks 5–11, in parallel), each following the recipe of the earlier countries (Slovenia's
commit `f0ffbc1` is the model), merged one by one with `tests/run.sh` green after each merge (Task 12).

**Tech Stack:** Godot 4 / GDScript, JSON data in `data/`, tests in `tests/` run headless by `tests/run.sh`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-09-batch4-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Each country on its own map, folders `hu`, `ro`, `bg`, `md`, `gr`, `cy`, `mt`; no routes between countries.
- Moldova includes Transnistria (Tiraspol, Bender as map cities), but on the road it is plain Moldova: Moldovan
  signs, leu at stations, no checkpoint, no border.
- Cyprus: only the Republic-controlled south. No Kyrenia, Famagusta, TRNC flag, name or crossing. The north is
  plain land without cities, roads or label. Cyprus' only neighbour is Greece (never Turkey).
- Cyprus and Malta: `drive: "left"`.
- Greece, Bulgaria, Cyprus: bilingual signs, native script on top, Latin underneath. City `name` stays in Latin
  letters (map, menus, HUD).
- Ferries: Greece Peiraiás – Irákleio (Crete), Malta Ċirkewwa – Mġarr (Gozo). Stop at the quay, ~3 s ferry
  screen (`FERRY_TIME := 3.0`), not counted against the time limit, no fuel used.
- Every postcard its own new painter (no borrowed drawings); one postcard per map city; no town postcards.
- No anthems, no new interface languages. Texts for the player in all of `GameData.LANGUAGES`
  (pl, en, cs, is, de); facts written as in earlier countries.
- Commit messages in the repo's style (long English sentence listing what came in), ending with
  `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.
- Never delete the user's Godot `profile.json`; previews back it up and restore it.

## Review Focus

1. Driving past the quay without braking: the car must stop at the quay barrier (a crash, like a roadside
   obstacle), never drive into the sea or skip the ferry. Test in Task 1.
2. A ferry in the `b -> a` direction (Heraklion -> Piraeus) and on left-hand roads (Gozo -> Malta): mirrored km,
   quay on the correct side. Test in Task 1 (direction) and Task 2 (left-hand scenery).
3. A trip through a ferry leg without stopping at it as its end city (e.g. Chania -> Athína through Irákleio):
   the ferry sits mid-trip after a map city; time limit fair. Test in Task 1 with a 3-city fixture.
4. Pausing or the time running out during the 3 s ferry: pause freezes it, time never runs out on board. Test
   in Task 1.
5. Bilingual board with a town missing from `native.json`: one Latin line, no empty line, no crash. Test in Task 3.

### Task 1: Ferry logic (Route + Race)

**Files:**
- Modify: `src/logic/route.gd` (build, new constants and `ferry_at`)
- Modify: `src/logic/race.gd` (stop at the quay, the crossing, timer frozen)
- Modify: `data/README.md` (roads.json `ferry`)
- Test: `tests/test_ferry.gd` (new; register in `tests/run_tests.gd` as the other tests are)

**Interfaces:**
- Produces, data: roads.json road field `"ferry": {"from_km": float, "to_km": float, "name": String, "fact": texts}`
  (the sea between the two quays, km from `a`; the driven road ends at `from_km` and resumes at `to_km`).
- Produces, Route: constants `FERRY_QUAY := 160.0` (track metres of quay before the barrier), `FERRY_GAP := 60.0`
  (track metres from the barrier to the far quay's ramp end). `stage.ferries: Array` of
  `{"z": float (barrier), "z_out": float (= z + FERRY_GAP, where the car resumes), "name": String, "fact": String,
  "sea_km": float}`. `static func ferry_at(stage: Dictionary, z: float) -> Dictionary` (the ferry whose quay
  `[z - FERRY_QUAY, z_out]` contains z, or `{}`).
- Produces, Race: `const FERRY_TIME := 3.0`; `var ferry_left := 0.0` (> 0 while on board); `signal ferry_started(ferry: Dictionary)`,
  `signal ferry_ended`.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing test** `tests/test_ferry.gd`, fixture `zz_ferry` built like `test_borders.gd`'s
  (Poland's country.json as base): cities `aa` (Alfa), `bb` (Beta), `cc` (Gamma); roads `aa–bb` 400 km, road
  `"A1"`, `ferry: {from_km: 100, to_km: 380, name: "Alfa – Beta"}`, zones covering 0..400; `bb–cc` 60 km. Checks:
  - `Route.build("aa","bb")`: `ferries.size() == 1`; `length` equals `Route.track_length(400 - 280)` within 1 m
    (the sea is not driven); `ferries[0].z` within 1 m of `100 * m_per_km`; `z_out - z == FERRY_GAP`.
  - `Route.build("bb","aa")`: one ferry at `20 * m_per_km` (mirrored km).
  - `Route.build("cc","aa")` (3-city, ferry mid-trip after Beta): one ferry, after Beta's town z.
  - Race on `aa -> bb`: put the car at `z = ferries[0].z - 50`, speed 0 → after one step `ferry_left` is
    `FERRY_TIME` and `ferry_started` fired; stepping 3.1 s leaves `time_left` unchanged and fuel unchanged, then
    `z == z_out`, `speed == 0`, `ferry_ended` fired.
  - Race at `z = ferries[0].z - 30`, speed 25 m/s, holding gas: within 2 s `z <= ferries[0].z` and `last_crash`
    was set (hit the barrier), and then once stopped the ferry starts.
  - Pausing (not calling `step`) does not change `ferry_left`; `state` stays `"running"` while on board even
    with `time_left` at 0.1.
- [ ] **Step 2: Run** `tests/run.sh` — expect FAIL in test_ferry (no `ferries` key).
- [ ] **Step 3: Implement in `Route.build`**: for each leg's `ferry` (km mirrored for `b -> a` as tunnels are),
  subtract its sea km from the km that sets `length`, map every km after `to_km` onto the track as
  `z(km) = (km - sea_km) * mpk + FERRY_GAP` for that leg and later ones, and drop towns/zones/tunnels/borders inside
  the sea. Keep stations, boards, crossings and tunnels out of `[z - FERRY_QUAY - 40, z_out + 40]` (add to `avoid`).
  `_legal_time` adds 10 s per ferry (braking and boarding). `stage.km` stays the real total (shown on the map).
- [ ] **Step 4: Implement in `Race`**: a barrier at `ferry.z` behaves like a roadside obstacle across the whole
  road (crash rules: stop dead, `CRASH_FUEL`, `last_crash`); when `speed < STOP_SPEED` with `z` in
  `[z - FERRY_QUAY, z]`, start the crossing: `ferry_left = FERRY_TIME`, emit `ferry_started`; while
  `ferry_left > 0`, `step` only counts it down (no physics, no fuel, no `time_left`, no traffic, no police
  chase continuing: a chase ends on boarding); at 0 set `z = z_out`, `speed = 0`, `x` to the driving lane,
  emit `ferry_ended`. Traffic gets no cars between `z - FERRY_QUAY` and `z_out`.
- [ ] **Step 5: Run** `tests/run.sh` — expect all PASS.
- [ ] **Step 6: Document** `ferry` in `data/README.md` (roads.json section).
- [ ] **Step 7: Commit** (`src/logic/route.gd src/logic/race.gd src/logic/traffic.gd tests/test_ferry.gd tests/run_tests.gd data/README.md`).
